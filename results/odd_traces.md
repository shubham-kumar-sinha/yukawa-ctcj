- *Inversion tensor* (`numerator_transport`, `run_class_odd(line=k)`): the grade-3 parts x, y of the two traces
  are paired through the conformal structure of the line, x.(2 n^(n_|y) - y) with n the unit vector along it
  (= x.y in D = 3); its Fourier transform gives [(2 + ep) x.y - 2 (p_|x).(p_|y)/p^2]/(1 - ep).
- *Fierz* (`run_class_odd(fierz=True)`): Tr A Tr B = [Tr(AB) + Tr(A g_m B g_m)]/2 for 2x2 matrices, cut at the
  reading points, with the even trace taken in D dimensions.  By g_m Gamma^(k) g_m = (-1)^k (D - 2k) Gamma^(k)
  this is sum_k (1 + 2k - D)/2 <A_k, B_k> over the grades k = 1, 3, 5 of the two chains: in D = 3 only k = 3
  survives; in D dimensions the grade-3 pairing (which is the DREG_3 eps~ eps~ = det pairing) is multiplied by
  (7 - D)/4, and grades 1 and 5 add evanescent terms.

- inversion tensor: line-independent for each of the twelve classes: **PASS**
- inversion tensor = ep/(1 - ep) x DREG_3 read at the same line, for all 36 (class, line), through ep^0 (ep/(1 - ep) = (4 - D)/(D - 2): 1 at D = 3, 0 at D = 4): **PASS**
- hence the inversion-tensor sewing has no pole: it gives no O(eps^3) term at all: **PASS**
- Fierz, single classes: 1/ep^3 poles from the evanescent grades 1 and 5: **PASS**
- Fierz, <JJ>: a class minus its partner with one loop reversed (cuts mapped r -> {0: 1, 1: 0, 2: 4, 3: 3, 4: 2}), i.e. the combinations that multiply I_0 and I_1, equals (7 - D)/4 x the same combination in DREG_3 through 1/ep, for all 11 cut pairs computed (same-line and others): the evanescent grades cancel: **PASS**
- Fierz, <TT>: the sum of the six classes, each averaged over its three lines (reversal maps the lines of a class onto those of its partner, so the evanescent grades cancel), equals (7 - D)/4 x DREG_3 same-line through 1/ep (a fixed line label in every class is not reversal-consistent and leaves 1/ep^4 poles): **PASS**

So both sewings reproduce the structure of the same-line rule (grade-3 pairing at the two ends of one line,
independent of the line), each with its own factor f(D), f(3) = 1.  The O(eps^3) term is the residue at D = 4 and
scales with f(4):

| continuation | f(D) | kappa_T | Delta C_J / C_J,psi |
|---|---|---|---|
| DREG_3 (eps~ eps~ = det) | 1 | 7/6 - zeta3 | (-3) I_0 + (2 - 6*zeta3) I_1 |
| Fierz into one even trace | (7 - D)/4 | 7/8 - 3*zeta3/4 | (-9/4) I_0 + (3/2 - 9*zeta3/2) I_1 |
| inversion tensor | (4 - D)/(D - 2) | 0 | (0) I_0 + (0) I_1 |

The sewing therefore does not derive the same-line rule: the cut points are a choice in every formulation
(the Fierz form at other cuts is (7 - D)/4 x DREG_3 at those cuts), and the normalisation f(4) is a further
choice.  f(4) = 1 is Zerf et al.'s DREG_3 normalisation, the one for which their four-loop Delta_3 restores
supersymmetry; the Fierz form multiplies the grade-3 pairing, hence the simple pole of any odd-odd diagram with
only grade-3 contributions, by 3/4.
