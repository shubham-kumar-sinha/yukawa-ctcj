# yukawa-ctcj

**C_T and C_J of general scalar–fermion (Yukawa) CFTs in d = 4 − ε, both through O(ε³).**

Authors: Shiroman Prakash and Shubham Sinha. If you use this code or its results, please cite the paper and this repository as described in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).

This repository computes the stress-tensor and current two-point coefficients at *any* fixed point of the general renormalizable scalar–fermion theory

$$
\mathcal L=\tfrac12(\partial\phi_a)^2+\bar\psi\,\partial\!\!\!/\,\psi+\phi_a\bar\psi y^a\psi+\tfrac1{24}\lambda_{abcd}\phi_a\phi_b\phi_c\phi_d,
$$

in the conventions of Jack, Osborn and Steudtner ([arXiv:2301.10903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10903)). The results come from all diagrams of ⟨TT⟩ and ⟨JJ⟩ through four loops, reduced with [FORM](https://github.com/form-dev/form) and [Forcer](https://github.com/benruijl/forcer). They are expressed through coupling-tensor invariants with ε-independent rational coefficients.

The accompanying paper is S. Prakash and S. Sinha, *C_T and C_J of general scalar–fermion conformal field theories in d = 4 − ε* (arXiv identifier to be added). Everything in it — every formula, table, figure and check — is regenerated by this repository; [`METHOD.md`](METHOD.md) maps each item of the paper to the script that produces it.

**Reproducing the paper.** One command reruns every step from scratch in a fresh copy and compares the results with the published ones:

```sh
pip install -r requirements.txt
python3 reproduce.py                  # from the cached Feynman integrals (about 40 min on one core)
python3 reproduce.py --integrals 8    # also recompute every integral with FORM/Forcer (see INSTALL_FORM.md)
```

[`METHOD.md`](METHOD.md) explains each step with its formulas: diagram generation and symmetry factors, integrals, flavor factors, the Fourier transform, extracting C_T and C_J, renormalization and the fixed-point form. `python3 scripts/walkthrough_one_loop.py` runs all of these steps on the one-loop diagrams, printing every intermediate quantity, and recovers the textbook free-field C_T and C_J in general d.

## Main results

Conventions:

- Couplings are rescaled as y → 4πy and λ → 16π²λ.
- `Tr` is the fermion-loop trace (tr 1 × flavor trace), normalized so that γ_φ^(1)ab = ½ Tr(yᵃyᵇ). N_f four-component Dirac fermions have Tr 1 = 4N_f.
- The symmetry is δψ = iTψ, δφ = tφ, with Ward identity i[yᵃ,T] = t_ab yᵇ. The paper writes (T, t) as (Θ, θ).
- C_{T,S} = d/((d−1)S_d²) and C_{J,ψ} = 1/S_d².

At any fixed point, through O(ε²):

```
C_T / C_{T,S} = n_s + (d-1)/2 Tr1 - 5/4 Tr(y^a y^a)
              - 1/6 Tr(y^a y^b)Tr(y^a y^b) - 11/48 Tr(y^a y^a y^b y^b) - 19/6 Tr(y^a y^b y^a y^b)
              - 5/36 λ_abcd λ_abcd

C_J / C_{J,ψ} = Tr(TT) + t_ab t_ab/(d-2) - 3/2 Tr(TT y^a y^a)
              - Tr(TT y^a y^a y^b y^b) - 17/2 Tr(TT y^a y^b y^a y^b) - 5/8 Tr(TT y^a y^b y^b y^a)
              + 3/4 Tr(T y^a T y^a y^b y^b) + 15/2 Tr(T y^a T y^b y^a y^b) + 3/2 Tr(T y^a y^b T y^b y^a)
              - Tr(TT y^a y^b)Tr(y^a y^b) + 3/2 Tr(T y^a T y^b)Tr(y^a y^b)
              - 3/8 t_ae t_bf λ_abcd λ_efcd
```

The two-loop invariants Tr(yᵃyᵃ) and Tr(TTyᵃyᵃ) are evaluated with y* to O(ε²); everything else at the one-loop fixed point.

At O(ε³) the four-loop terms add, to C_T/C_{T,S} (a word like `aabcbc` stands for Tr(yᵃyᵃyᵇyᶜyᵇyᶜ)):

```
 + (1/72 + 9ζ3/8) Tr(aabbcc) + (21ζ3/2 − 49/24) Tr(aabcbc) + (9ζ3/8 − 289/576) Tr(aabccb)
 + (11ζ3 − 119/18) Tr(abacbc) − (11/4 + 15ζ3/4) Tr(abcabc)
 + (21ζ3/8 − 37/32) Tr(aabc)Tr(bc) + (23ζ3/4 − 187/288) Tr(ab)Tr(acbc) + (3ζ3/4 − 31/576) Tr(ab)Tr(ac)Tr(bc)
 + 127/36 λ_abcd Tr(abcd) + 253/1728 λ_acde λ_bcde Tr(ab) − 7/36 λ_abcd λ_abef λ_cdef
 + (7/6 − ζ3) Tr(abc)Tr(abc)
```

and, to C_J/C_{J,ψ}, the 52 non-zero four-loop terms of paper eq. (D.1) plus the two odd-loop terms below. y* is then needed to O(ε³) in the two-loop invariants, and λ* to O(ε²).

- **Machine-readable form.** The four-dimensional-algebra coefficients are in `results/coefficients*.json` and frozen in `ctcj/formulas.py` and `ctcj/formulas4.py`; the odd-loop coefficients are `KAPPA_T_ODD` and `KAPPA_J_ODD` in `ctcj/formulas4.py`. `matrix/ct3.tex` and `matrix/cj3.tex` are the paper's O(ε³) equations verbatim, including the odd-loop terms. The ⟨JJ⟩ basis (48 y⁶, 6 y⁴λ, 4 y²λ², 3 λ³) and its 87 Ward relations are in `data/basis4_J.json` and `data/relations4_J.json`.
- **The last C_T term and the corresponding C_J terms** come from fermion loops with an odd number of Yukawa vertices. They vanish for every theory obtained from four dimensions, and in three-dimensional-only theories they are fixed by 𝒩=1 supersymmetry ([`ODD_LOOPS.md`](ODD_LOOPS.md)).

## Evaluating any theory

`ctcj/theory.py` evaluates C_T and C_J for any user-specified theory: several Yukawa and quartic couplings, every fixed point (multi-start Newton, so increase `starts` for many couplings), stability, and a Ward-identity check of each current. The three-loop β_y, γ_φ, γ_ψ of JOS are in `ctcj/betas3.py` and the two-loop β_λ in `ctcj/betasJ.py`.

```python
from ctcj.theory import Theory
th = Theory(Y=[...], L=[...], mult=...)       # invariant Yukawa and quartic tensors, as in examples/06_custom_theory.py
fp = [f for f in th.fixed_points() if f["stable"]][0]
th.CT3(fp)             # (1, c1, c2, c3):  C_T/C_T,free = 1 + c1 eps + c2 eps^2 + c3 eps^3
th.CJ3(fp, T, t)       # the same for C_J of the current (T, t); odd=True adds the odd fermion loops
```

Worked examples, each under a minute, are in `examples/`: chiral Ising (N = 1–16), chiral XY with the 𝒩=2 test, chiral Heisenberg, two fermion species, chiral O(n) GNY with Clifford Yukawas, and a template with two quartics.

## Cross-checks

All run automatically and all pass:

- **Free fields.** C_T and C_J of free fermions, improved scalars and charged scalars are exact in d.
- **Poles and logs.** All poles cancel separately in every invariant, and log μ²x² drops out at fixed points, at every order.
- **Known results.** O(n) Wilson–Fisher through ε³ (C_T from Osborn–Stergiou, C_J from the analytic bootstrap); large-N Gross–Neveu C_T1 and C_J1 through ε³ (Diab et al.); FGKT at O(ε).
- **𝒩=2 supersymmetry.** C_J^R/C_J,free^R = C_T/C_T,free through ε³ at chiral XY with N = 2, and there C_J/C_J,free of the U(1) current equals 9(d−2)/((d−1)(d+2)) C_T/C_T,free exactly.
- **𝒩=1 supersymmetry.** The supercurrent and the fermionic partner of a flavor current, computed through four loops with their own diagrams, projectors and Fourier transforms, give C_S = C_T at five points and C_j = C_J at two ([`ODD_LOOPS.md`](ODD_LOOPS.md)).
- **Inputs.** The ⟨JJ⟩ Ward relations hold on random U(1) and SU(2) theories. The three-loop β-functions of JOS reproduce Zerf et al. and 𝒩=1 relations on random superpotentials; changing any of their 81 coefficients fails a check.
- **Independent paths.** The two-loop and large-N ⟨TT⟩ values match an earlier, partially independent computation (reference values in `ctcj/checks.py`). `matrix/` evaluates the formulas exactly as printed in the paper.
- **Expansion depth.** Forcer depth 4 gives the same coefficients as depth 3 on 73 four-loop classes.

## Quick start and full reproduction

The per-diagram integrals are cached in `data/`, so no FORM installation is needed for the following:

```sh
pip install -r requirements.txt
python3 scripts/compute_results.py      # C_T, C_J through O(eps^2), models, checks (~3 min)
python3 scripts/compute_results4.py     # C_T at O(eps^3) + checks
python3 scripts/compute_results4J.py    # C_J at O(eps^3) + checks (~6 min)
python3 scripts/paper_tables.py         # chiral models, N=2 R-current, conductivities, 1/N^2 (~3 min)
```

Recomputing the integrals needs [FORM](https://www.nikhef.nl/~form) 5.0.2 and [Forcer](https://github.com/benruijl/forcer); [`INSTALL_FORM.md`](INSTALL_FORM.md) has instructions for Linux, macOS and Windows (WSL):

```sh
sh scripts/setup_form_forcer.sh            # FORM 5.0.2 (form-bin) + Forcer, then an exact-reproduction test
python3 scripts/test_form_setup.py         # recomputes three four-loop diagrams, demands exact agreement
python3 reproduce.py --integrals 8         # everything, including every integral
```

## Caveats

- **Three-dimensional-only theories.** The odd fermion loops are computed in a continuation that 𝒩=1 supersymmetry selects. For non-supersymmetric 3d-only theories the same continuation is used, as part of the scheme. The odd invariants must be evaluated with the theory's actual three-dimensional flavor structure ([`ODD_LOOPS.md`](ODD_LOOPS.md)).
- **γ₅.** Chirality is carried by the flavor matrices (JOS convention). Parity-odd pieces of a single fermion loop cannot contribute to ⟨TT⟩ or ⟨JJ⟩ at this order; this is argued, not computed.
- **β-functions.** MS-bar throughout. The three-loop γ_φ and γ_ψ are taken in their symmetric form, as in Zerf et al.
- **Padé estimates.** The quoted d = 3 ranges are the spread of pole-free approximants, not error bars. The 𝒩=2 point, where the answer is exact, is the calibration.
